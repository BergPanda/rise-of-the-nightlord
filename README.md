## Requirements
This project is not here to replace Elden Ring: The Board Game, Dark Souls:
The Board Game, or any of their countless Core Sets, Addons, Expansions,
or Mega-bosses.

The basegame of Elden Ring: The Board Game or any of its Core Sets are strictly
required in order to play Rise of the Nightlord. To expand immersion even further,
it is recommended to also get the basegame of Dark Souls: The Board game or any
of its Core Sets, Addons, Expansions, or Mega-bosses. The more products are
available to you, the more features and aspects of Rise of the Nightlord will
be unlocked. Therefore, it is recommended to get as many of the Elden Ring:
The Board Game or Dark Souls: The Board Game products as you can!

## Elden Ring: The Board Game - Rise of the Nightlord
This project serves as a custom ruleset that is meant to emulate an
Elden Ring: Nightreign like playing experience.

To accomplish the playing experience of Elden Ring: Nightreign,
this project tries to translate three main aspects of Elden Ring: Nightreign
into Elden Ring: The Board Game. All necessary changes and additions to the rulebook
to link these aspects of Elden Ring: Nightreign to Elden Ring: The Board Game are
bundled within the documents of this project.

### The connection between Elden Ring and Dark Souls
One of the main aspects of Elden Ring: Nightreign is its connection of the world
of Elden Ring to the different worlds and timelines of the Dark Souls trilogy.
Luckily for this project, Dark Souls: The Board Game already exists, which
simplifies the integration the aforementioned connections into Elden Ring:
The Board Game. In this project, all the numerous different bosses of
Dark Souls: The Board Game and its products are converted to the ruleset of
Elden Ring: The Board Game and the extended rules of Rise of the Nightlord.
The individual Dark Souls: The Board Game bosses are available in the form of
cards, that can be used in Elden Ring: The Board Game. However, some aspects of
these bosses like the miniature itself, for example, require the products, that
these bosses originate from in order to integrate them in their entirety.

### The randomly generated world
The second aspect of Elden Ring: Nightreign this project tries to emulate is the
compact game world of each run of Elden Ring: Nightreign filled with random enemies,
bosses, events, and landmarks. These random aspects can be translated into Elden
Ring: The Board Game with relative ease. All the different tiles of all available
products of Elden Ring: The Board Game can be combined and placed accordingly to
recreate a compact game world not unlike that of Elden Ring: Nightreign.
The playing area is placed in a very different way to the original scenarios of
Elden Ring: The Board Game and brings a breath of fresh air to the existing ruleset.

### The rogue-like gameplay and permanent rewards
Finally, Elden Ring: Nightreign offers a vastly different gameplay
experience to that of FromSoftware's previous titles like Elden Ring or Dark Souls.
In Elden Ring: Nightreign must survive in an ever-changing rogue-like world,
full of traps, hard bosses, and difficult challenges. Each new run resets the
players back to zero, which makes each enemy a new challenge in and of itself.
To help and motivate players long-term, Elden Ring: Nightreign offers permanent
rewards that provide benefits for each consecutive run to reach new hights.
In order to implement these gameplay aspects of Elden Ring: Nightreign into Elden
Ring: The Board Game, numerous systems and cards are created to emulate the
rogue-like experience and rewarding gameplay of Elden Ring: Nightreign.

## Contents of Rise of the Nightlord
This project offers a variety of completely reworked or entirely new cards,
that can be integrated into Elden Ring: The Board Game seamlessly. The contents
of this project are designed to be printed on paper or cards. It is recommended
to use the contents of this project physically in order to shuffle and pick random
cards out of decks just like in the original Elden Ring: The Board Game.
Nevertheless, the contents of this project can also be used digitally provided
a solution card shuffling and random card selections are implemented.
Such solutions are not provided by this project!
